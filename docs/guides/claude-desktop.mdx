Pitboard switches Claude Desktop on macOS only. On Windows and Linux it leaves Claude Desktop alone and [refuses to enrol a Claude Desktop account](/reference/errors#claude-desktop).

Pitboard changes Claude's login only while Claude is closed. On the command line, quit Claude before each command on this page. In the app, Pitboard offers to quit Claude for you, and opens it again once the change is made.

## Before you add a Claude Desktop account

- Quit Claude with Command-Q. Closing its window leaves it running.
- A Claude Desktop label starts with `desktop/`, as in `desktop/work`. In the app, choose **Claude Desktop** as the tool and enter the name alone.
- [What Pitboard does not do for Claude Desktop](/concepts/claude-desktop#what-pitboard-does-not-do-for-claude-desktop) says how these accounts differ from Claude Code's.

<Warning>
  Do not choose **Log out** in Claude: it ends the login Claude holds at claude.ai, and Pitboard cannot bring it back.
</Warning>

## Add the account Claude is signed in to

Open Claude, check which account it shows, then quit it. Pitboard cannot ask claude.ai whose a login is.

In the app, click Pitboard's item in the menu bar, then choose **Add Account**. Choose **Claude Desktop** in the **Tool** pop-up menu, enter a name such as `work`, then click **Add Account**. If Claude is open, click **Quit Claude and Add**. If the dialog says **Pitboard can't find Claude Desktop on this Mac.**, open Claude and sign in once, then return to the dialog.

On the command line:

```sh
pitboard enroll desktop/work
```

If Claude has not finished signing in, Pitboard enrols nothing. Open Claude, wait until it shows your chats, quit it, then try again. If Claude is signed out, add the account as [Add another account](#add-another-account) shows.

## Add another account

Pitboard parks the account in use, which leaves Claude signed out and signs nothing out at claude.ai. You sign in to the other account inside Claude, and Pitboard adds it once Claude quits.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    Click Pitboard's item in the menu bar, then choose **Add Account**. Choose **Claude Desktop** in the **Tool** pop-up menu, then enter a name such as `travel`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Park the account in use">
    Click **Park work**, or **Start Sign-In** while Claude is signed out. If Claude is open, Pitboard asks to quit it first: click **Quit Claude and Park work** or **Quit Claude and Start Sign-In**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open Claude if it is closed, and sign in to the account you are adding. When it shows your chats, quit Claude with Command-Q. The dialog closes once Pitboard has added the account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On the command line, run the command, then do in Claude what it says:

```sh
pitboard enroll desktop/travel --sign-in
```

```text
Parked `desktop/work`. Claude is signed out, and nothing was signed out at claude.ai.
Open Claude and sign in to the account to add as `desktop/travel`. When Claude shows your chats, quit it with Command-Q.
Do not choose Log out in Claude: it ends the login Claude holds at claude.ai, and Pitboard cannot bring it back.
Waiting for Claude to quit. Control-C stops waiting and keeps `desktop/work` parked; `pitboard use desktop/work` puts it back.
Claude is open.
Enrolled desktop/travel, the account Claude is signed in to now.
```

If Claude quits before the sign-in has finished, Pitboard says so and keeps waiting: [`enroll`](/reference/account-commands#enroll) lists each line. If Claude is signed in to an account you have already added, Pitboard adds nothing and names the command to run.

Pitboard refuses to park an account it has not enrolled. Add that account first, as [Add the account Claude is signed in to](#add-the-account-claude-is-signed-in-to) shows.

An add you leave with **Later** or Control-C keeps Claude signed out: [Finish or undo an add or a sign-in again](/troubleshooting/claude-desktop#finish-or-undo-an-add-or-a-sign-in-again) says what to do.

## Switch accounts

In the app, click Pitboard's item in the menu bar, then choose the Claude Desktop account. If Claude is open, click **Quit Claude and Switch** when Pitboard asks. Pitboard opens Claude again behind the Pitboard window. If Claude has not quit within 30 seconds, nothing changes.

On the command line:

```sh
pitboard use desktop/home
```

```text
Switched to desktop/home; desktop/work is parked.
Claude opens signed in to it.
```

If Pitboard says Claude is open, or that part of it is still running, nothing has changed. [Claude is open](/troubleshooting/claude-desktop#claude-is-open) says what to do.

## Sign in again

Sign in again when an account shows **Needs signing in again** or `nothing parked`, or when Claude asks you to sign in after a switch. It runs as [Add another account](#add-another-account) does: in the Pitboard window, Control-click `home` and choose **Sign In Again**, then follow its steps 2 and 3, signing in to `home`.

On the command line, run the command, then sign in to `home` in Claude and quit Claude with Command-Q:

```sh
pitboard enroll desktop/home --sign-in
```

```text
Signed in to desktop/home again. Claude is signed in to it now.
```

Where the account had a parked login, Pitboard sets it aside and deletes nothing. If Claude is signed in to another account when it quits, Pitboard records nothing for `home` and says why.
