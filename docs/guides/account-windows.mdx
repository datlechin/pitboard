Each window keeps its own website data, so your accounts stay signed in side by side. A Claude Code account's window is on claude.ai, a Codex account's on chatgpt.com. [Where account windows keep their data](/security#where-account-windows-keep-their-data) says where it is and when Pitboard deletes it.

## Open an account window

In Pitboard's menu in the menu bar, choose **Open claude.ai as work**. When the site has several accounts, choose **Open claude.ai**, then the account. In the Pitboard window, Control-click an account and choose **Open claude.ai** or **Open chatgpt.com**.

A window's title is its account's label. Renaming the account keeps the window signed in.

Links to other sites open in your default browser. Anything you connect there goes to the account your browser is signed in to.

[Account windows](/reference/account-windows#account-windows) lists every place a window opens from, and [Downloads](/reference/account-windows#downloads) where it saves files.

## Sign in

The first time Pitboard opens an account's window, a note at its top names the email address to sign in with and the steps.

Google's sign-in does not work. Google does not allow it inside apps, so the window stops it.

### claude.ai

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the email">
    Enter the email address the note names, then click **Continue with email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the link on your phone">
    Open the email on your phone and tap its link. On this Mac, the link would open in your browser instead of the window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code">
    In the window, enter the code claude.ai shows on your phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### chatgpt.com

Enter the account's email address, then its password or the code chatgpt.com emails you. Or click **Continue with Microsoft** or **Continue with Apple**.

Passkeys do not work. macOS offers them to a web page only in an app it lets act as a browser.

## What a window cannot do

- Gmail, Google Drive and Google Calendar cannot be connected, since connecting them needs Google's sign-in.
- In a claude.ai window, Google single sign-on does not work either.
- The window refuses the camera and the microphone, so voice mode does not work.
- Web notifications do not work.
- Pitboard cannot see who is signed in to a window. Its title names the account it belongs to.
- A window stays with its own account after a switch.

## Open a page from your browser

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share the page">
    In Safari, click **Share** in the toolbar or choose **File** > **Share**. In Chrome or Firefox, choose **File** > **Share**. Then choose **Pitboard**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the account">
    In the **Open Link** window, select an account. Pitboard first selects the one you chose last for that site, or else the account in use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Open">
    The page opens in that account's window. If the window was already open, **Back** returns to what it showed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Pitboard refuses a page on another site with **Can't Open This Link**. It refuses a site's sign-in link too, which would sign the window in as whoever the link belongs to. [Refused links](/reference/account-windows#refused-links) lists every case.

With no account on the page's site, the **Open Link** window shows **No claude.ai Account**. Click **Add Account**, and the link waits there until you choose the account you added.

If **Pitboard** is not in the Share menu, see [Pitboard is missing from the Share menu](/troubleshooting/account-windows#pitboard-is-missing-from-the-share-menu).

## Sign a window out

Bring the account's window to the front, choose **File** > **Remove Website Data**, then click **Remove**. Pitboard deletes the cookies and everything else the site keeps in that window. The window starts again at the site's home page, with the sign-in note.

The site is not told, so the account stays signed in on your other devices and browsers.

Forgetting the account, in the app or with `pitboard forget`, deletes its window's data too.
