---
title: "Add and manage accounts"
description: "Enrol the account each tool is signed in to, add more through a sign-in, and rename or forget them."
---

Pitboard switches only between accounts you enrol. Each one gets a label, the name you switch to it by.

## Add the account you are signed in to

For OpenAI's Codex CLI, check [Before you enrol a Codex account](/guides/codex#before-you-enrol-a-codex-account) first.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="App">
    In the **Accounts** pane of the Pitboard window, click **Name** in the tip **Give this account a name**. Enter a name such as `personal`, then click **Save**.

    The tip shows a Codex login only once a Codex account is enrolled. To enrol your first one, follow [Add Codex accounts](/guides/codex#add-codex-accounts).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Command line">
    ```sh
    pitboard enroll personal          # Claude Code
    pitboard enroll codex/personal    # Codex
    ```

    ```text
    Enrolled personal (me@example.com), the account signed in now.
    Add another without signing out of it: pitboard enroll <label> --sign-in
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Enrolling this account parks nothing. Pitboard parks its login the first time you switch away, as [Parked logins](/concepts/switching#parked-logins) explains.

Enrolling a Claude Code login that Pitboard has not identified before needs the network, to ask Anthropic which account it is.

## Add another account

Pitboard runs the tool's own sign-in in a private directory. The account in use stays signed in.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="App">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the dialog">
        Click Pitboard's item in the menu bar, then choose **Add Account**. If the dialog has a **Tool** pop-up menu, choose **Claude Code** or **Codex**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Name the account">
        Enter a name such as `work`, then click **Sign In**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign in">
        In your browser, sign in as the account you are adding. If the browser does not open, click **Open Sign-In Page**. If a **Code** field appears, paste the code your browser shows, then click **Submit Code**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    If Codex is not offered, see [Add Codex accounts](/guides/codex#add-codex-accounts).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Command line">
    ```sh
    pitboard enroll work --sign-in          # Claude Code
    pitboard enroll codex/work --sign-in    # Codex
    ```

    When your browser opens, sign in as the account you are adding.

    ```text
    Enrolled work (me@company.com). Switch to it with: pitboard use work
    ```

    For what `--sign-in` runs, see [`enroll`](/reference/account-commands#enroll).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

If the browser signs in to an account already enrolled under another label, Pitboard refuses and names that label.

Pitboard counts each organisation your Anthropic account belongs to as a separate account. Enrol each under its own label, and sign in to that organisation in the browser. Your personal ChatGPT plan and each workspace you belong to are separate Codex accounts in the same way.

<Warning>
  Do not add an account with Claude Code's `/login` or with `codex login`. Each replaces the login in use without Pitboard parking it first. The account that was in use then needs a browser sign-in. `codex login` also revokes the login it replaces: see [Why Codex logins are moved, not copied](/concepts/switching#why-codex-logins-are-moved-not-copied).
</Warning>

## Choose a label

A label is one word, such as `work`. When you enrol an account, `work` names a Claude Code account and `codex/work` a Codex one. In the app, choose the tool and enter the name without `codex/`.

Other commands take `work` alone while only one tool has a `work` account. Otherwise, write `claude/work` or `codex/work`. [Labels](/reference/commands#labels) has the full rules.

## Rename an account

Renaming changes only the label. The account keeps its parked login, and its [account window](/guides/account-windows) stays signed in.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="App">
    In the **Accounts** pane, Control-click the account, then choose **Rename**. Enter the new name, then click **Rename**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Command line">
    ```sh
    pitboard rename wrong right
    ```

    ```text
    Renamed wrong to right (me@company.com).
    ```

    A rename stays inside its tool: `pitboard rename codex/work job` gives `codex/job`.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Forget an account

Forgetting an account deletes its parked login. It also deletes everything its [account window](/guides/account-windows) keeps on this Mac, its sign-in included. To forget the account in use, switch to another account first.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="App">
    In the **Accounts** pane, select the account and press Delete, or Control-click it and choose **Forget**. Click **Forget** to confirm.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Command line">
    ```sh
    pitboard forget old
    ```

    ```text
    Forget old and delete its parked login? Adding it again needs a browser sign-in. [y/N] y
    Forgot old (old@example.com).
    ```

    To skip the question, add `--yes`.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Pitboard does not revoke the login it deletes. That login stays valid until it expires: see [What Pitboard does not protect against](/security#what-pitboard-does-not-protect-against).
