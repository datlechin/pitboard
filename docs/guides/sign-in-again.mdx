---

Signing in again to a Claude Code or Codex account runs the tool's own sign-in in your browser, as adding an account does. The account keeps its label and its account window.

A Claude Desktop account signs in again inside Claude, as [Use Pitboard with Claude Desktop](/guides/claude-desktop#sign-in-again) shows.

## Signs that an account needs it

`pitboard` shows one of these after the account's email, then `pitboard enroll work --sign-in`:

- `login expired`
- `nothing parked`
- `login replaced outside Pitboard`

In the app, Pitboard's menu marks the account **Needs signing in again**, and its row in the Pitboard window and a notification offer **Sign In Again**. [Account status](/reference/account-status) says what each means.

A switch to such an account moves nothing and names the same command:

```text
error: the parked login for `work` has expired. Run `pitboard enroll work --sign-in` to sign in to it again.
```

A line that starts `ready · expires in` needs no browser: `pitboard renew` renews that login.

On Windows, a switch that refuses with `credential_sealed` needs a sign-in again too: see [Parked logins do not open on this PC](/troubleshooting/recovery#parked-logins-do-not-open-on-this-pc).

## Sign in again

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="macOS">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the dialog">
        Click Pitboard's item in the menu bar, then choose the account marked **Needs signing in again**. Or, in the Pitboard window, Control-click the account and choose **Sign In Again**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Start the sign-in">
        In the **Sign In to work Again** dialog, click **Sign In**. Your browser opens the tool's sign-in.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign in">
        Sign in as that account. The dialog closes once the tool has the login.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Windows">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the dialog">
        In the Pitboard window, select **Sign in again** in the account's row. Or right-click the account and select **Sign in again**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Start the sign-in">
        In the **Sign in to work again** dialog, select **Sign in**. Your browser opens the tool's sign-in.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign in">
        Sign in as that account. The dialog closes once the tool has the login.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

If the browser does not open or a **Code** field appears, see [Add another account](/guides/accounts#add-another-account). On the command line, write `codex/work` for a Codex account:

```sh
pitboard enroll work --sign-in
```

```text
Opening Claude Code's sign-in. Sign in as me@company.com; the account in use now stays signed in.
```

After the sign-in, it prints `Renewed work (me@company.com): its parked login is a fresh one.` If you sign in as another account, Pitboard keeps nothing from that sign-in:

```text
error: `work` already refers to me@company.com. Choose a different label.
```

Start again and sign in as `me@company.com`.

## Sign in again to the account in use

The new login takes the place of the one the tool has, and Pitboard parks nothing. In the app, sign in again from the account's shortcut menu, as for any account. After the sign-in, a notice titled **work has a new login** appears.

On the command line, `pitboard enroll work --sign-in` ends with:

```text
Signed in to work (me@company.com) again. Its new login is the one in use now.
```

Everything that runs Codex keeps the old login until it starts again, and can write it back over the new one when it renews. Restart each as [Restart what runs Codex](/guides/codex#restart-what-runs-codex) says. Pitboard lists the ones still running.

## If Pitboard cannot identify the login in use

Pitboard replaces the login in use only once it has identified it. When it cannot, it parks the new login, and the tool keeps the one it has. That happens when:

- The login in use cannot be read or found.
- A Claude Code login changed since Pitboard last identified it, and Anthropic no longer accepts it or does not answer.

[How Pitboard knows whose login it is](/concepts/identity) says when Pitboard asks Anthropic. If `work` is the account Pitboard last recorded in use, it also warns with `sign_in_parked_not_in_use`, which says what to do if the tool's login no longer works. In the app, the notice is titled **The new login was parked, not put in use**.

To keep parked logins from expiring, see [Keep parked logins alive](/guides/renewal).
