On the command line, run `pitboard`, the same as `pitboard status`:

```sh
pitboard
```

```text
● personal  me@example.com  Max 20x  signed in
    5h    ██████░│░░   59%  resets in 1h 10m  18% under pace
    week  █│█████░░░   73%  resets in 5d 18h  55% over pace
          weekly limit runs out in 11h 05m at this pace

○ work      me@company.com  Max 20x  ready · good for 26d 4h
    5h    █░░░│░░░░░   12%  resets in 3h 00m  28% under pace
    week  ████░░│░░░   40%  resets in 2d 4h   29% under pace
```

With accounts of more than one tool, each tool has its own heading and its own account in use. [Account status](/reference/account-status) says what each mark, colour and line means, and what to do about each.

## See usage in a widget or from Shortcuts

On macOS, Pitboard's widgets and the Shortcuts action **Get Usage** show usage without opening Pitboard. On Windows, Pitboard has neither.

To add a widget, Control-click the wallpaper, choose **Edit Widgets**, search for `Pitboard`, then click **Account** or **Accounts**. For Notification Center, click **Edit Widgets** at its foot instead. **Account** shows the account in use: to show another, Control-click the widget, choose to edit it, then choose the account. On macOS 26 and later, the control **Account** shows how much of its fullest limit an account has used, in Control Center or the menu bar: click Control Center in the menu bar, click **Edit Controls**, then add it.

A widget shows what Pitboard last read, so its numbers move only while Pitboard runs. [Widgets and controls](/reference/mac-app#widgets-and-controls) says what each one shows.

In Shortcuts, or in Spotlight on macOS 26 and later, run **Get Usage**. With no account chosen, it gives the accounts in use, a line each, such as **work: 5-hour 42% used, Weekly 12%**, and hands a shortcut [each account's details](/reference/mac-app#shortcuts-and-spotlight).

## Read without asking, or ask again

To see the last numbers measured, without asking Anthropic or OpenAI, run `pitboard status --offline`. It renews no parked login, and each Claude Code or Codex account ends with a line such as `measured 14:02, 3m ago · read without asking Anthropic`.

To ask Anthropic and OpenAI again, even about an account asked moments ago, run `pitboard status --fresh`, or choose **Refresh** in Pitboard's menu (Command-R). On Windows, select **Refresh** in the Pitboard window, or press F5 there.

When Pitboard cannot get newer numbers, it shows the last ones with when and why, as in `measured 14:02, 3m ago · Anthropic could not be reached`. The app shows the reason under the account in the window. [Usage shows old numbers](/troubleshooting/accounts#usage-shows-old-numbers) says what to do.

## Choose what the menu bar shows

On macOS, choose from the **Menu Bar Shows** pop-up menu in **Settings** > **General** to show the percentage alone or the icon alone. [Settings](/reference/settings#menu-bar-shows) lists each choice.

On Windows, the icon has no such setting. Its lit row shrinks as one limit of the account in use fills, as [Icon in the notification area](/reference/windows-app#icon-in-the-notification-area) says.
