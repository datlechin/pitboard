The menu bar app gives each enrolled Claude Code account a claude.ai window of its own. Each window keeps its own website data, so every account stays signed in side by side, with no browser profile and no signing out.

You sign in on claude.ai's own page, and WebKit keeps that sign-in on your Mac, as a browser would, in a [folder for each window](/reference/files#claude-ai-windows). pitboard never reads, copies or changes it, and never makes a claude.ai sign-in from a Claude Code login.

## Open an account's window

In pitboard's menu, choose **Open claude.ai as work**. With two or more Claude Code accounts, choose **Open claude.ai**, then the account's label. In the pitboard window, you can also Control-click an account and choose **Open claude.ai**.

Each account has one window, titled with its label. Choosing the account again brings its window to the front. A window you close and open again starts at claude.ai's home page.

While a claude.ai window is open, pitboard has a Dock icon, a place in the Command-Tab switcher and its menus in the menu bar. The **File** menu then lists the accounts too, and the **View** menu has **Back**, **Forward** and **Reload Page**. pitboard leaves the Dock when you close its last claude.ai window.

A Codex account, a Claude Code login with no name in pitboard and a login pitboard cannot use have no claude.ai window. To give a login one, name it as [Add the account you are signed in to](/guides/accounts#add-the-account-you-are-signed-in-to) describes.

## Sign in

The first time a window opens, a bar at its top names the account to sign in as.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for the email">
    On claude.ai's page, click **Continue with email** and enter the account's address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the link on another device">
    Open the email claude.ai sends on your phone and tap its link. claude.ai shows a code there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code">
    Enter the code in the window. The window stays signed in from then on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Anthropic's help centre says a link opened on another device shows a code. One report says a browser on the same Mac is signed in instead, so tap the link on your phone, not on this Mac.

Google blocks its sign-in inside apps, so the window stops **Continue with Google** and says to use email. An account made with Google signs in by email with the same Gmail address.

## Open a claude.ai link as the account

In the window, choose **File** > **Open Link** (Command-L), paste the link, then click **Open**. The link opens in that window, signed in as its account. To open a link from a browser as the account you choose, see [Open claude.ai links as an account](/guides/claude-links).

Links to anywhere but claude.ai open in your default browser. When claude.ai sends a page there by itself, such as a sign-in to connect an integration, the window says so. The browser connects it to the claude.ai account the browser is signed in to.

## Download and upload files

Downloads go to your Downloads folder, and the bar at the top of the window says when each one finishes. A download that an artifact or another site starts asks first, with **Download** and **Cancel**. A download goes on after you close its window.

claude.ai's own upload button opens the Open dialog.

If a page does not load, the bar says why. Choose **View** > **Reload Page** to try again.

## Sign out of one window

Choose **File** > **Sign Out of This Window**, then click **Sign Out**. pitboard stops the window's downloads, deletes everything the window keeps for the account, and opens claude.ai again, signed out.

<Warning>
  Do not use claude.ai's own **Log Out** to sign one window out. It signs the account out on every device and browser.
</Warning>

claude.ai is not told, so the sign-in stays listed under claude.ai's **Settings** > **Account** until it expires or you end it there.

Forgetting a Claude Code account in pitboard deletes its window's data too. After `pitboard forget` on the command line, the app deletes it at its next read.

## What a window does not do

- Switching Claude Code changes no window. The claude.ai sign-in and the Claude Code login are separate, and neither is made from the other.
- Google sign-in, Google single sign-on and connecting Gmail, Google Drive or Google Calendar are stopped, since Google blocks its pages inside apps.
- Single sign-on through another identity provider, and connecting another integration such as GitHub, open in your browser. They sign in or connect the claude.ai account that browser has, not the window's.
- Voice mode does not work: the window refuses the microphone and the camera.
- Web notifications do not appear. WebKit has no public way for an app to show them.
- WebKit's own **Download Linked File** and **Download Image** are not in the shortcut menu. Use claude.ai's own download buttons.

For every command, message and shortcut of a claude.ai window, see [Menu bar app](/reference/app#claude-ai-windows).
