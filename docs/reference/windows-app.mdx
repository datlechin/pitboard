The app on Windows runs from its icon in the notification area, on Windows 11 24H2 or later. It does what [the menu bar app](/reference/mac-app) does, in sentence case, and has no Claude Desktop accounts. This page gives where it differs.

Its windows are the Pitboard window, each [account's window](/reference/account-windows#account-windows) and the [**Open link** window](/reference/account-windows#open-link-window). Closing the Pitboard window leaves Pitboard running, and **Exit** in its menu stops it. While a download runs, **Exit** asks first.

Opened from the Start menu, Pitboard opens its window. Started when you sign in to Windows, it opens none. Opened while it runs, it brings its window to the front.

## Icon in the notification area

Windows puts a new icon in the overflow, behind the arrow at the end of the taskbar. Drag it onto the taskbar to keep it in sight.

| The icon shows | When |
| --- | --- |
| Its lit row whole | The limit the [menu bar item](/reference/mac-app#menu-bar-item) would show is under 70% used. |
| Its lit row half lit | That limit is from 70% used. |
| A sliver of its lit row | That limit is from 90% used. |
| Its row unlit | Nobody is signed in, or that limit has no reading. |
| Windows' warning symbol at its bottom right | [The menu](#menu) opens on something that needs attention. |

A click, or Enter on the icon, opens the Pitboard window. A right-click, or Shift+F10 on the icon, opens Pitboard's menu. To reach the icon from the keyboard, press Win+B, then the arrow keys. While the icon is in the overflow, first press Enter on the arrow to open the overflow.

Its tooltip reads **Pitboard**, then **Needs attention** while the menu opens on something to look at, then a line for each account in use: its label and the line [the menu](#menu) shows under it, such as **work: 5-hour 42%, weekly 12%**. With several tools, each label names its tool: **work (Claude Code): 5-hour 42%**. Windows shows the first 127 characters.

## Menu

Pitboard's menu lists what the [menu bar app's menu](/reference/mac-app#menu) lists, without Claude Desktop, and choosing an item does the same. Where the menu bar app shows a line under an item, this menu shows it as the item's tooltip.

A dot marks the account in use in each tool. The percentage the [menu bar item](/reference/mac-app#menu-bar-item) would show of an account comes after it: **work · Max 20x · 42%**. An account whose tooltip says what stands in its way, such as **Needs signing in again**, shows none.

**Open Pitboard** is bold: a click on the icon does the same.

## Window

The Pitboard window's navigation pane holds **Accounts**, **Activity** and **This PC**, with **Settings** at its foot. The window opens on **Accounts**, at the place and size it had when you last closed it. In a narrow window, the pane folds behind a button in the title bar.

### Accounts

**Accounts** lists what the menu bar app's [Accounts pane](/reference/mac-app#accounts-pane) lists, without Claude Desktop.

The setup tip **Start when you sign in to Windows** stands in for **Open Pitboard at login**. It shows while Windows does not start Pitboard when you sign in, and **Settings** > **Apps** > **Startup** has not turned it off. It says where Windows puts the icon, and its **Taskbar settings** opens Windows' taskbar settings, where **Other system tray icons** can keep the icon in view.

### Activity

**Activity** lists what the menu bar app's [Activity pane](/reference/mac-app#activity-pane) lists, with dates in your PC's format.

### This PC

**This PC** runs the checks of [`pitboard doctor`](/reference/maintenance-commands#doctor) each time you show it, with doctor's summary line under its title.

When Windows refused the last notification Pitboard posted, **Notifications** follows the checks with its title, the time and the error Windows gave, as its exception's type and HRESULT. It goes once a notification is posted, and the summary line counts it.

## Accounts and dialogs

An account's shortcut menu offers what the menu bar app's does, and leaves out an item that cannot be chosen. Its dialogs and alerts say what the menu bar app's say, with **on this PC** for **on this Mac**.

## Notifications

The app posts the notifications the [menu bar app](/reference/mac-app#notifications) lists, as Windows notifications, with the same titles, bodies and buttons. Selecting a notification opens what it opens on macOS, with **Settings** scrolled to **Limits** in place of its **Limits** pane.

Windows can turn them off in **Settings** > **System** > **Notifications**. Where Windows shows none of Pitboard's notifications, Pitboard's **Settings** says why under **General**, as [Notifications in Settings](/reference/settings#notifications) lists.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Keys | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ctrl+N | The Pitboard window | Opens **Add account** |
| Ctrl+Comma | The Pitboard window | Shows **Settings** |
| F1 | The Pitboard window | Opens this site, as **Pitboard help** does |
| F5 or Ctrl+R | **Accounts**, **Activity** and **This PC** | **Refresh**, or **Check again** in **This PC** |
| F6, Shift+F6 | The Pitboard window | Moves between the navigation pane and the page |
| Ctrl+W | The Pitboard window | Closes it. Pitboard keeps running. |
| Ctrl+W | An account window, outside its page | Closes it |
| Alt+Left, Alt+Right | An account window | **Back**, **Forward** |
| F5 or Ctrl+R | An account window | **Reload** |
| Enter, or double-click | A row in **Accounts** | What the row's button does. On the account in use when it is not enrolled, opens **Name this account**. |
| Shift+F10, or the menu key | A row in **Accounts** | Opens its shortcut menu |
| Delete | A row in **Accounts** | **Forget**, after asking |
| F2 | A row in **Accounts** | **Rename** |
| Ctrl+C | A row in **Accounts** | **Copy email address** |
| Ctrl+C, Ctrl+A | What a check found, in **This PC** | **Copy**, **Select all** |
| Enter | A dialog | Its default button |
| Escape | A dialog | **Cancel**, where it is not dimmed |
